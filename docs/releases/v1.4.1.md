Tala 1.4.1 improves the meeting notebook on macOS.

- **Show transcript stays within reach.** The transcript bar stays at the bottom of the session as you scroll. Choose **Show transcript** to open the transcript and bring it into view.
- **Preview enhanced notes as they’re written.** Open **Enhanced notes · live** during enhancement to see notes as they arrive. You can keep writing in **Your notes** while Tala works. If enhancement is canceled or fails, the previous saved version remains available.
- **More room for notes.** The notebook uses a more compact layout, with copy, export, and session settings in the session actions menu.

## Downloads

- **Tala.dmg** for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14.2 or later. Drag Tala to Applications. The ad-hoc signed app may require approval in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- **tala-linux-x86_64.tar.gz** for Linux x86_64, built on Ubuntu 24.04. Extract the archive and keep `tala-tools` next to the `tala` executable.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** contains checksums for both packages.
